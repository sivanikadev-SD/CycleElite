Frequently Asked Questions
How do I sync my Garmin or Wahoo devices?
You can connect your devices through the 'Sensors' section in your Settings. We support direct integration with Garmin Connect, Wahoo Cloud, and Strava.
How can I export my performance data?
On the Analytics page, click the 'Export' button in the top right corner. You can download your data in .CSV, .FIT, or .TCX formats.
Can I share my training plan with a coach?
Yes, if you have a premium subscription, you can invite your coach via the 'Team' tab in Settings. They will then have read-access to your workouts and analytics.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Settings > Billing & Plans. There you can see your current plan and find the option to cancel or downgrade.
